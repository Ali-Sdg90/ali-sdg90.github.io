With a lot of careful tweaking, I finally got the card angles right!

I kept thinking that if the angles were wrong, it would be the first thing people noticed, even if they couldn't explain why. It could undermine all the work I'd already done and everything still ahead. If something looked off, users would immediately feel that the scene wasn't natural.

I also realized that each row needed a different card height.

Then I added infinite scrolling, acceleration, natural drag behavior, and shadows at both ends of each row.

The movement speed and the friction when throwing the cards finally felt natural and fun.
