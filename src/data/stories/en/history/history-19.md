One feature that came to me halfway through the project, and quickly became really important to me, was this history of how I built it.

It was like a project diary for myself, while letting other people follow the way I thought: how the project grew, what problems I ran into, and how I solved them.

To me, one of the most important skills for a software engineer is learning how to see a problem and approach solving it. In a way, I wanted to give that journey to anyone viewing the project. A funny little gift: two months of Ali's work packed into a few images :)

I wanted it to have its own page, but I spent a lot of time thinking about where its button should go.
I didn't want it to disappear into the page or demand too much attention.
Every part of the layout already had a reason to be there, so I couldn't just drop a random button somewhere. Eventually, I came up with two ideas, which you'll see in the next two slides.
