For testing, I took a photo of myself wearing the same outfit and using the same lighting angle as the design, then placed it in the project.

It was honestly disappointing to realize that my photo didn't work well on the page.

The lighting angle, image quality, and overall realism all needed to be perfect. If even one part felt slightly off, the image stopped looking natural and made the whole page less comfortable to look at. I realized that continuing with this design would put the entire project at a high risk of failure.

There was another problem too. My photo drew too much attention. The shelf was supposed to be the focal point, and having two parts of the page constantly competing for attention wasn't good for the experience.

So it was a failed but useful experiment, and I knew I needed to change the design.
