I put together an initial design for the section's Expanded state and added a subtle blur effect. It turned out really cool because I could see the cards moving behind the section. I also softened the design of the button used to expand it, although it was still a little bulkier than what I wanted for the final version.

This was just an experiment to see how the section would look and behave. It didn't have a scrollbar yet, the text had a bit too much spacing on the right, and I was also planning to increase the font size in the Expanded state later.

But I really liked the section's size, how it behaved, and especially that blur effect. The visual test had passed in my eyes, so I could move on to implementing it more fully and cleaning up the remaining details.
