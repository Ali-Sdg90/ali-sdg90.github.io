In the greatest design tool of all time, MS Paint, I sketched a gallery-style layout for the images and text.

I didn't want users to scroll through one long page, then keep nudging the scrollbar up and down just to line each image and paragraph up with their view. If I'd kept that design, they probably would have given up on reading the page.

With this model, everything would live inside one defined frame. Users could choose which part to view, which language to read, and how quickly to move through the sections.

The idea felt right, so I started building it.
