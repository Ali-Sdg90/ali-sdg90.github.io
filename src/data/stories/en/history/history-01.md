I'd always wanted a personal portfolio: one place to show everything I'd built, but not just another plain page. I wanted it to feel completely like Ali, something that made it obvious from the start that it came from my own creativity and personality.

Something like a hub: a central place for my projects, resume, social links, and everything else connected to me.

When life got a little quieter and I felt ready to begin, I had the idea of building a bookshelf where each book represented one of my projects. A simple, cozy bookshelf.

I even thought it could be fun to have a stop-motion version of me standing beside it, waving, following the cursor with his eyes, with a few small easter eggs hidden around the page.

I sketched the first version of the idea in Paint, and that became the project's first rough design.
