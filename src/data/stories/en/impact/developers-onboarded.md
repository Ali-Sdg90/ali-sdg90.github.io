At MelkRadar, after my role changed from Front-End Developer to Front-End Technical Lead, one of the responsibilities I took on was onboarding new members of the frontend team.

These developers could be joining different projects across the company or working on multiple projects at the same time. MelkRadar already had an established onboarding process, documentation, and training videos, but new developers still needed someone to guide them through everything and help them get comfortable with the team, projects, and workflows.

I was responsible for onboarding 11 new frontend developers.

I held several sessions with each of them, introduced them to the relevant projects, helped them get the access they needed, and explained our coding standards and how the team worked. I also worked through some of their first tasks with them through pair programming, from thinking through a problem and creating a branch to implementation, testing, opening a Pull Request, and getting familiar with the actual development workflow.

One of the things that mattered most to me was creating a safe and comfortable environment for them. I wanted them to feel free to ask questions, experiment with different approaches, and not be afraid of making mistakes. I wanted them to know that whenever they ran into a problem, there were people on the team they could reach out to, including the person onboarding them.

I really enjoyed this responsibility too. I've always liked teaching, and seeing someone gradually grow in something I've helped them learn, become comfortable in a new environment, and eventually work independently is something I genuinely enjoy.

I'm happy I had the opportunity to help these 11 developers get started and grow at MelkRadar. I also got to meet some really nice people and make quite a few friends along the way. :)
