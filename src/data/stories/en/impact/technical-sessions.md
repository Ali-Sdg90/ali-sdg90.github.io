For around two years, I was a technical mentor in the CS Internship program and held technical sessions for web development interns.

These sessions had their own weekly time slot. Interns could register for them and join the session to show me their posts, activities, projects, or anything they had questions about. I would review their work, talk through the problem with them, and guide them through it.

The sessions were usually around an hour long.

One of my favorite parts of being in the program was these weekly sessions. Every week I got to sit down with interns, see what they had been working on, hear their questions, and talk through the things they were dealing with. I really enjoyed these sessions.
