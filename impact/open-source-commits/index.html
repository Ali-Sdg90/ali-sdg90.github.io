Story

I've also been pretty active in open-source projects.

Most of that work and most of my commits were related to CS Internship. The program itself was open source, and many of the projects and tools we built for it were hosted in its GitHub Organization.

Part of my work involved creating and improving courses and educational materials for different parts of the program. Another part was developing tools and bots that were actually used inside the program, like CS Club Bot, CS Queue Bot, CS Queue Calendar, and other tools we built to make things easier.

Outside of CS Internship, I also contributed to other open-source projects like fx-blox, TonRich, and a few smaller ones.

I've always enjoyed open source and teamwork. I like the idea of people with different experiences and ideas working together on the same project and moving it forward. I've had some really good experiences with this kind of collaboration, and I'd like to do more of it in the future. :)
