# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Please keep all participation respectful, constructive, and focused on improving
the project.

Expected behavior includes:

- Giving clear and considerate feedback.
- Respecting different perspectives and experience levels.
- Respecting privacy, copyright, attribution, and personal boundaries.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project-related discussions.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.

To report a conduct concern privately, email
[ali.sdg.dev90@gmail.com](mailto:ali.sdg.dev90@gmail.com). Reports will be
handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
